APPLICANT PROTOCOL FAQ
Can I apply if I only have an idea without a working MVP? expand_more
Yes for the FastTrack Incubator and Fellowship tracks. We evaluate technical feasibility, founder clarity, and the structural depth of the problem space. For the flagship $25k Genesis Pitch Bowl, having a live proof-of-concept, clickable prototype, or initial user signals significantly strengthens your jury score.
What does "equity-free" really mean? Are there hidden SAFEs or warrants? expand_more
100% non-dilutive. The grant funds are disbursed via the university venture endowment. EDC takes zero equity, zero governance rights, and zero advisory shares. If you decide to raise subsequent priced rounds, you retain your entire cap table intact.
Can cross-institutional teams apply together? expand_more
Yes. As long as at least one co-founder is currently matriculated or a recent graduate of an accredited university program, your entire team is eligible for competition stages and incubator residency.
What happens after submitting this form? expand_more
Every submission triggers a hash receipt sent to your verified .edu email. Within 72 hours, our screening committee reviews the materials and updates your dossier status. Candidates moving to Phase 2 receive an invite to book a partner screening sprint.
